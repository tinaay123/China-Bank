Background & History

Maybank Philippines Inc. (MPI) is a full-service commercial bank that was established in 1997 after the acquisition of the former PNB Republic Bank by the Maybank Group.

As part of the Maybank Group, Malaysia's largest financial services group and one of the leading banking groups in Southeast Asia, MPI benefits from the expertise and resources of a premier financial institution that serves over 22 million customers through a network of over 2,600 offices in 18 countries.

Over the years, Maybank Philippines has expanded its operations and services, emerging as a significant player in the Philippine banking sector with a focus on providing innovative financial solutions to individuals and businesses.

Corporate Profile

Maybank Philippines Inc. (MPI) is a commercial bank established in 1997 as part of the Maybank Group's expansion into the Philippine market. Operating under the supervision of the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), MPI provides a full range of banking services to individuals, small and medium enterprises, and large corporations in the Philippines.

Ownership Structure

Maybank Philippines is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Malayan Banking Berhad (Maybank), Malaysia's largest financial services group and one of the leading banking groups in Southeast Asia.

Subsidiaries & Affiliates

  • Maybank ATR Kim Eng Securities Inc. - Provides securities brokerage, investment advisory, and corporate finance services in the Philippines
  • Maybank ATR Kim Eng Capital Partners Inc. - Involved in investment banking, corporate finance, and financial advisory services
  • AsianLife and General Assurance Corporation - An affiliate offering various insurance products in partnership with Maybank
  • Maybank International Labuan Branch - Supports offshore banking activities and international operations in coordination with Maybank Philippines

Maybank Philippines maintains a wide network of ATMs located at our branches and strategic locations nationwide. Our ATMs are part of the BancNet and Megalink networks, allowing our customers to access their accounts through thousands of ATMs across the Philippines.

Digital Banking

In addition to our physical branches, we offer comprehensive digital banking services through our Maybank2u online banking platform and mobile app, enabling customers to perform banking transactions anytime, anywhere.

Business Scope & Focus

Corporate Banking

Maybank Philippines provides comprehensive corporate banking solutions to large corporations, multinational companies, and medium-sized businesses. Our services include working capital financing, trade finance, project financing, and corporate loans.

Commercial Banking

Our commercial banking division focuses on serving the needs of small and medium enterprises (SMEs) through tailored financial solutions including business loans, cash management services, and trade financing facilities.

Retail Banking

Maybank offers a wide range of personal banking services including savings and current accounts, personal loans, credit cards, mortgages, and investment products designed to meet the financial needs of individual customers.

Global Banking

Leveraging the Maybank Group's extensive regional network, we provide cross-border banking services, international remittances, and foreign exchange solutions to facilitate our clients' international business operations.

Capital Management Strategy

Maybank Philippines implements a comprehensive capital management strategy focused on:

  • Maintaining strong capital ratios well above regulatory minimums to support business growth and absorb potential shocks
  • Optimizing capital allocation across business segments to maximize risk-adjusted returns
  • Regular stress testing to assess capital adequacy under various adverse scenarios
  • Proactive capital planning aligned with the bank's strategic objectives and expected growth
  • Compliance with Basel III requirements and Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas regulations
